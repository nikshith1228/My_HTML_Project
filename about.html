About My Project

Welcome to ScoreScale—a lightweight, easy-to-use CGPA calculator built for Scaler students. Whether you're tracking your PSP scores, mid-terms, or final projects, ScoreScale converts your continuous assessment marks into an accurate CGPA estimate so you can focus on learning and building without the hassle of manual calculations.

In this project, I learned many new things and grew by learning from my mistakes.
